# Code of Conduct

**Document owner:** Legal and Compliance
**Version:** 2026.1
**Effective date:** 1 January 2026
**Applies to:** All employees, contractors and interns

## 1. Our commitment

VeriDox is committed to a workplace where people are treated with dignity and
respect. This Code sets the minimum standard of behaviour expected of everyone
who represents the company.

## 2. Professional behaviour

- Treat colleagues, customers and partners with courtesy.
- Communicate honestly and keep commitments.
- Protect company property and use it responsibly.
- Follow the law in every jurisdiction where we operate.

## 3. Anti-harassment and non-discrimination

Harassment of any kind is prohibited. This includes unwelcome comments or
conduct based on gender, age, race, religion, caste, disability, sexual
orientation or any other protected characteristic.

Complaints may be raised with a manager, People Operations or the anonymous
ethics hotline. Complaints are investigated promptly and confidentially. The
company prohibits retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good
faith.

## 4. Conflicts of interest

Employees must disclose outside employment, board positions, or financial
interests that could conflict with company interests. Disclosure is made to the
manager and Legal within 30 days of the change.

Employees may not use their position to obtain personal benefit from suppliers,
customers or competitors.

## 5. Gifts and hospitality

Employees may accept gifts of nominal value. A gift with a value above **USD 50**
(or the local equivalent) must be declined or handed to Legal for donation. Any
gift that could reasonably influence a business decision must be declined
regardless of value.

Business meals and hospitality must be reasonable, infrequent and approved by a
manager. Gifts to government officials are never permitted.

## 6. Confidentiality and insider information

Employees must protect confidential information during and after employment.
Trading in company securities while in possession of material non-public
information is prohibited.

## 7. Use of company assets and social media

Company assets, including brand, systems and data, must be used for legitimate
business purposes. Employees may discuss their work publicly only if the
information has been approved for publication. Customer names and personal data
must never be shared on social media.

## 8. Speaking up

Employees are encouraged to raise concerns early. Reports can be made to any
manager, People Operations, Legal or the anonymous hotline. The company will not
tolerate retaliation. Investigations follow a documented process and aim to
conclude within 30 days.

## 9. Consequences

Breaches of this Code may lead to coaching, formal warning, loss of bonus,
termination or referral to authorities, depending on severity. Managers who
fail to act on reported concerns are themselves subject to disciplinary action.

## 10. Acknowledgement

All employees must acknowledge this Code within 14 days of joining and annually
thereafter. Contractors acknowledge the relevant sections in their agreements.

## 11. Questions

Questions about this Code can be sent to legal@veridox.example or raised with
People Operations. This Code is reviewed annually by Legal and Compliance.
