GLOBAL TRAVEL AND EXPENSE POLICY 2026
Document owner: Finance
Effective from: 1 January 2026
Applies to: All employees and contractors

SECTION 1: SCOPE

This policy applies to all business travel and business expenses incurred on behalf of VeriDox Technologies. Employees must obtain line manager approval before booking travel. Travel for amounts above 1,500 US dollars also requires finance approval.

SECTION 2: AIR TRAVEL

Employees must book economy class for flights shorter than six hours. Premium economy may be used for flights longer than six hours with manager approval. Business class requires approval from a director and is limited to flights longer than nine hours.

Air travel must be booked through the approved corporate travel portal. Personal frequent flyer benefits may be retained, but tickets must not be upgraded at company expense without written approval.

SECTION 3: HOTEL ACCOMMODATION

The nightly hotel limit is 220 US dollars in tier-one cities and 160 US dollars elsewhere. Employees should choose properties from the approved hotel list where available. Exceptions require manager approval and a written justification.

Hotel extras such as minibar, entertainment and spa services are not reimbursable.

SECTION 4: PER DIEM AND MEALS

The standard daily meal allowance for domestic and regional corporate travel is capped at 75.00 US dollars per diem. International travel has a per diem of 110.00 US dollars per day.

The per diem covers breakfast, lunch and dinner. Alcohol is not reimbursable. Eligible expenses require digitised itemised receipts submitted within thirty business days. If a meal is provided by the company or included in a conference fee, the per diem is reduced by 25 percent for that meal.

SECTION 5: GROUND TRANSPORT

Airport transfers must use approved ground transport providers or public transport. Ride-hailing services are permitted when public transport is unsafe or impractical. Taxi and ride-hailing receipts are required.

Personal vehicle mileage is reimbursed at 0.62 US dollars per mile. Parking and tolls are reimbursable with receipts.

SECTION 6: REIMBURSEMENT PROCESS

Employees must submit expenses through the finance system within 30 business days of the expense date. Each claim must include the business purpose, cost centre, itemised receipts and the names of attendees for meals.

The line manager approves the claim and finance performs the final review. Reimbursements are paid with the next payroll cycle after approval. Claims submitted more than 60 days after the expense may be declined.

SECTION 7: CORPORATE CARDS

Corporate cards may be issued to employees who travel regularly. Cardholders must reconcile transactions within 15 days. Personal use of a corporate card is prohibited. Lost or stolen cards must be reported to the card provider and finance immediately.

SECTION 8: NON-COMPLIANT EXPENSES

Expenses that do not comply with this policy may be rejected or recovered from the employee. Repeated non-compliance may result in card privileges being withdrawn and disciplinary action.

Questions should be directed to the finance team at finance@veridox.internal.
