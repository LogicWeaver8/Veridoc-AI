# LEAVE POLICY 2026

**Document owner:** People Operations
**Effective from:** 1 January 2026
**Supersedes:** Leave Policy 2025
**Applies to:** All permanent employees

## 1. Purpose

This policy explains the leave entitlements and approval process for VeriDox employees for the 2026 calendar year. It replaces the Leave Policy 2025.

## 2. Annual leave

Employees receive 18 days of annual paid leave per calendar year. Annual leave accrues monthly and may be used as soon as it is accrued. Requests must be submitted at least 10 business days before the first day of leave and require approval from the employee's line manager.

A maximum of 8 unused annual leave days may be carried over into the next calendar year. Carried-over days must be used by 31 March or they will lapse.

## 3. Sick leave

Employees receive 12 days of paid sick leave each calendar year. A medical certificate is required for any absence longer than two consecutive working days. Managers must be informed before 10:00 on the first day of absence.

Sick leave may not be used as a substitute for annual leave. Long-term medical absences are managed under the company's health and wellbeing procedure.

## 4. Public holidays

The company observes the public holidays published by the state in which the employee is based. If an employee is required to work on a public holiday, a compensatory day off will be provided within 60 days.

## 5. Parental leave

Employees are entitled to statutory parental leave in line with local law. In addition, the company provides 26 weeks of paid primary carer leave and 8 weeks of paid secondary carer leave. Parental leave must be requested at least 30 calendar days before the intended start date and is coordinated by People Operations.

## 6. Bereavement leave

Employees may take up to 5 days of paid bereavement leave for the death of an immediate family member. Additional unpaid leave may be approved by the line manager.

## 7. Unpaid leave

Unpaid leave may be requested for a maximum of 30 calendar days per year, subject to manager and HR approval. Requests must be submitted at least 15 business days in advance.

## 8. Approval process

All leave requests are submitted through the HR portal. The line manager must approve or reject a request within five business days. Approved leave is recorded in the HR system and cannot be cancelled without manager approval.

## 9. Contact

Questions about this policy should be sent to the People Operations team at people@veridox.internal.
