# Remote Work Standard Operating Procedure

**Document owner:** People Operations
**Version:** 2026.1
**Effective date:** 1 January 2026
**Applies to:** All permanent employees in India

## 1. Purpose

This SOP explains how employees request, record and manage remote work. It
supplements the Attendance and Working Hours section of the Employee Handbook.
Where the two conflict, the Employee Handbook governs working hours and this SOP
governs the remote-work approval process.

## 2. Eligibility

- Employees who have completed 90 days of continuous service are eligible.
- Employees on a performance improvement plan are not eligible until the plan is
  closed.
- Roles that require physical presence (facilities, hardware lab, reception) are
  not eligible.

## 3. Remote days allowance

Eligible employees may work remotely for up to **8 days per calendar month**.
Remote days do not carry over to the following month and unused days are not
paid out.

Managers may approve up to 2 additional remote days in a month for exceptional
circumstances such as a medical recovery plan. Approval must be recorded in the
HR portal and is valid for that month only.

## 4. Requesting remote work

1. Submit the request in the HR portal at least **2 business days** before the
   intended remote day.
2. Select the work location as "Remote — home" or "Remote — other".
3. The reporting manager must approve or reject the request within 1 business
   day.
4. For remote work from another city, also attach the travel reference if the
   company is paying for travel.

Same-day requests are allowed only for emergencies and require manager approval
by message followed by a portal entry within 24 hours.

## 5. Working hours while remote

Remote employees follow the same core hours as office employees: **10:00 to
16:00 IST**. All meetings with the team are held inside core hours. Outside core
hours, employees are expected to be reachable for urgent incidents if they are
on call.

Remote employees must record attendance in the HR portal before 11:00 IST on
each remote day.

## 6. Workspace requirements

- A reliable internet connection of at least 25 Mbps download and 5 Mbps upload.
- A private space where confidential calls cannot be overheard.
- Company laptop only. Personal devices may not be used to access company
  systems for remote work.
- Screen lock enabled after 5 minutes of inactivity.

Employees who cannot meet these requirements must work from the office or apply
for an exception through IT Security.

## 7. Data handling while remote

Remote employees must use the company VPN for every connection to internal
systems. Confidential documents may not be printed at home. Paper notes
containing customer data must be destroyed at the end of each working day.

Recording meetings that include customer data is prohibited unless all
participants have consented in writing.

## 8. Equipment and reimbursement

The company provides a laptop, headset and one external monitor. Employees may
claim a one-time home-office setup allowance of **INR 12,000** after completing
probation, with receipts submitted within 60 days of purchase.

Internet reimbursement is **INR 1,500 per month** for employees who work
remotely at least 4 days in that month. Claims are submitted through the
expense system with a service-provider invoice.

## 9. Non-compliance

Repeated failure to record attendance, unapproved remote work, or breaches of
the data-handling rules may result in withdrawal of remote privileges. Serious
security breaches are handled under the IT Security Policy and may lead to
disciplinary action.

## 10. Review

This SOP is reviewed annually by People Operations and IT Security. The next
review is due in December 2026.
