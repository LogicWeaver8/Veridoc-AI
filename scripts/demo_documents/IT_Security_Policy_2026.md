# IT Security Policy

**Document owner:** Information Security
**Version:** 2026.1
**Effective date:** 1 January 2026
**Classification:** Internal

## 1. Scope

This policy applies to every employee, contractor and intern who accesses
company systems, whether from a company device or an approved remote location.
It covers devices, accounts, data, networks and incident reporting.

## 2. Acceptable use

Company systems are provided for business use. Limited personal use is allowed
when it does not affect performance, security or storage capacity. The following
are prohibited:

- Installing unapproved software or browser extensions on company devices.
- Storing company data in personal cloud accounts.
- Sharing passwords, tokens or VPN credentials with anyone, including
  colleagues.
- Using company email to register for consumer services that store confidential
  data.

## 3. Passwords and authentication

- Passwords must be at least **14 characters** and unique to each system.
- Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for email, VPN, HR portal and code
  repositories.
- Passwords must be rotated immediately if a breach is suspected.
- A password manager is provided free of charge and is the approved place to
  store credentials.

Shared accounts are not permitted. Service accounts must be owned by a named
employee and reviewed every quarter.

## 4. Devices

All laptops must have full-disk encryption, automatic screen lock after 5
minutes, and endpoint protection installed and running. Devices must be enrolled
in the company device management system before first use.

Lost or stolen devices must be reported to IT Security within **1 hour** of
discovery through the security hotline. IT Security will remotely revoke access
and wipe the device.

Personal devices may access email and chat only through the company mobile
application with MFA enabled. Personal devices may not store company documents.

## 5. VPN and network

The company VPN is required whenever an employee accesses internal systems
outside a company office. Public Wi-Fi may be used only with the VPN active and
only for work that does not involve customer personal data.

Employees must not connect personal routers or network equipment to company
networks.

## 6. Data classification and handling

| Class | Examples | Storage | Sharing |
|-------|----------|---------|---------|
| Public | Marketing material, published policies | Any | Open |
| Internal | This policy, org charts, meeting notes | Company drives | Employees only |
| Confidential | Customer contracts, payroll, source code | Approved systems with access control | Named recipients only |
| Restricted | Personal data, authentication secrets, incident reports | Encrypted approved systems | Security team approval required |

Confidential and Restricted data must never be sent over personal email or
messaging apps. External sharing requires a signed data-handling agreement.

## 7. Email and phishing

Employees must report suspicious emails using the "Report phishing" button. Do
not forward suspected phishing messages to colleagues. The security team
publishes simulation results quarterly and will never ask for passwords by
email.

## 8. Software and AI tools

Only software from the approved catalogue may be installed on company devices.
Employees must not paste Confidential or Restricted data into public AI tools.
Approved AI assistants are listed on the intranet and are reviewed every six
months.

## 9. Incident reporting

Security incidents include suspected phishing, lost devices, accidental data
sharing, malware alerts and unauthorised access. Report incidents immediately to
security@veridox.example or the 24x7 hotline. Do not attempt to investigate or
remediate an incident yourself unless instructed by the security team.

The security team acknowledges reports within 1 hour and provides an initial
assessment within 1 business day. Post-incident reviews are completed within 10
business days.

## 10. Access reviews

Managers must review team access to Confidential and Restricted systems every
quarter. Access that is no longer required must be removed within 2 business
days of the review. Leavers lose all access on their last working day.

## 11. Compliance and consequences

Violations may lead to suspension of access, disciplinary action or termination
in line with the Code of Conduct and applicable law. Suspected criminal activity
is referred to the relevant authorities.

## 12. Exceptions

Exceptions to this policy require written approval from the Chief Information
Security Officer and must include a review date. Exceptions are logged in the
security register.
