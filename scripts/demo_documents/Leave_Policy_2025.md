# LEAVE POLICY 2025

**Document owner:** People Operations
**Effective from:** 1 January 2025
**Applies to:** All permanent employees

## 1. Purpose

This policy explains the leave entitlements and approval process for VeriDox employees for the 2025 calendar year. It applies to all permanent employees who have completed their probation period.

## 2. Annual leave

Employees receive 16 days of annual paid leave per calendar year. Annual leave accrues monthly and may be used as soon as it is accrued. Requests must be submitted at least 10 business days before the first day of leave and require approval from the employee's line manager.

A maximum of 5 unused annual leave days may be carried over into the next calendar year. Carried-over days must be used by 31 March or they will lapse.

## 3. Sick leave

Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave each calendar year. A medical certificate is required for any absence longer than two consecutive working days. Managers must be informed before 10:00 on the first day of absence.

## 4. Public holidays

The company observes the public holidays published by the state in which the employee is based. If an employee is required to work on a public holiday, a compensatory day off will be provided.

## 5. Parental leave

Employees are entitled to statutory parental leave in line with local law. Additional company parental leave may be approved by the Head of People on a case-by-case basis.

## 6. Unpaid leave

Unpaid leave may be requested for a maximum of 30 calendar days per year, subject to manager and HR approval. Requests must be submitted at least 15 business days in advance.

## 7. Approval process

All leave requests are submitted through the HR portal. The line manager must approve or reject a request within five business days. Approved leave is recorded in the HR system and cannot be cancelled without manager approval.

## 8. Contact

Questions about this policy should be sent to the People Operations team at people@veridox.internal.
