# EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK 2026

**Company:** VeriDox Technologies
**Owner:** People Operations
**Effective from:** 1 January 2026

## SECTION 1: WORKING HOURS AND ATTENDANCE

Core working hours are 10:00 to 16:00 local time, Monday to Friday. Employees are expected to be available for meetings and collaboration during core hours. Outside core hours, working patterns may be agreed with the line manager.

Employees must record attendance in the HR portal at the start and end of each working day. Attendance records are used for payroll, client billing and compliance audits.

Repeated lateness or unexplained absence must be discussed with the employee's line manager. Three or more unplanned absences in a rolling 30-day period require a documented check-in with People Operations.

## SECTION 2: LEAVE AND TIME OFF

Annual leave, sick leave, parental leave and bereavement leave are governed by the current Leave Policy. Employees should read the Leave Policy before submitting a request.

Public holidays follow the calendar for the employee's work location. The People Operations team publishes the holiday calendar every December.

## SECTION 3: REMOTE AND HYBRID WORK

Eligible employees may work remotely in accordance with the Remote Work SOP. Remote work is a privilege based on role suitability and performance, not an automatic entitlement.

Employees working remotely must remain reachable during core hours, attend scheduled meetings by video, and follow the information security requirements in the IT Security Policy.

## SECTION 4: WORKPLACE CONDUCT

Employees are expected to treat colleagues, clients and partners with respect. Harassment, discrimination, bullying and retaliation are prohibited. Concerns may be raised with a line manager, People Operations or through the confidential ethics line.

Conflicts of interest must be disclosed to the line manager. Employees may not accept gifts with a value above 50 US dollars from clients or suppliers without written approval.

## SECTION 5: PERFORMANCE AND DEVELOPMENT

Every employee participates in a twice-yearly performance review. Development goals are agreed with the line manager and recorded in the performance system.

The company supports professional development through the Learning and Development Policy. Training requests are subject to manager approval and available budget.

## SECTION 6: EXPENSES AND REIMBURSEMENT

Business expenses must be reasonable, documented and approved in advance when the amount exceeds 500 US dollars. Original itemised receipts are required for all reimbursement claims.

Travel and meal expenses follow the Travel and Expense Policy. Claims must be submitted within 30 business days of the expense being incurred. Claims submitted after 60 days may be rejected.

## SECTION 7: EQUIPMENT AND SECURITY

Company laptops, phones and access credentials remain company property. Employees must not install unapproved software on company devices or share credentials with anyone.

Lost or stolen devices must be reported to the IT Service Desk immediately. Security incidents must be reported within one hour in accordance with the IT Security Policy.

## SECTION 8: ACKNOWLEDGEMENT

By working at VeriDox Technologies, employees acknowledge that they have read and will comply with this handbook and the policies it references. Policies may be updated from time to time; the latest version in the document library takes precedence.
