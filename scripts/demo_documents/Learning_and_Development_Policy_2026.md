# Learning and Development Policy

**Document owner:** People Development
**Version:** 2026.1
**Effective date:** 1 January 2026
**Applies to:** All permanent employees who have completed probation

## 1. Purpose

We want every employee to keep growing. This policy explains the learning
budget, the approval process, study leave and certification support.

## 2. Annual learning budget

| Grade band | Annual budget (INR) |
|------------|---------------------|
| Band 1–2 (individual contributors) | 35,000 |
| Band 3–4 (senior individual contributors and managers) | 60,000 |
| Band 5 and above (directors and executives) | 100,000 |

The budget resets on 1 January each year. Unused budget may be carried forward
for **6 months** only, and only with manager approval recorded in the HR portal.

Budget covers course fees, certification exams, books, conference tickets and
approved online subscriptions. Travel for conferences is claimed separately under
the Travel and Expense Policy.

## 3. Requesting approval

1. Discuss the learning goal with your manager during a one-to-one.
2. Raise a request in the HR portal at least **10 business days** before the
   course start date.
3. Attach the course outline, cost and expected outcome.
4. The manager approves, then People Development checks budget availability.

Requests above INR 60,000 require an additional approval from the department
head. Requests are approved on a first-come basis within each team's budget.

## 4. Reimbursement

Submit the certificate of completion or attendance proof with the receipt within
**45 days** of completing the course. Reimbursement is processed with the next
payroll cycle.

If a course is not completed, the employee must inform People Development within
5 business days. Partial reimbursement may be approved for documented medical
reasons.

## 5. Study leave

Employees may take up to **5 days of paid study leave** per year for exams and
intensive workshops. Study leave is separate from annual leave and requires
approval at least 5 business days in advance. Unused study leave does not carry
over.

Exam days for professional certifications (for example PMP, CISA, AWS
certifications) may be taken as study leave even when the employer is not paying
for the exam, up to the 5-day limit.

## 6. Certifications and bonding

The company may fund high-value certifications above INR 100,000. Where funding
exceeds that amount, a repayment agreement applies: if the employee resigns
within 12 months of certification, a prorated amount is repayable. The exact
terms are stated in the approval letter.

## 7. Internal learning

Employees are encouraged to attend internal knowledge sessions, mentor
colleagues and contribute to documentation. Up to **8 hours per quarter** of
working time may be used for approved internal learning activities with manager
consent.

## 8. Non-compliance

Submitting false receipts or claiming for courses that were not attended is a
serious policy breach and is handled under the Code of Conduct. Unapproved spend
is not reimbursed.

## 9. Review

This policy is reviewed annually by People Development and Finance. The next
review is due in November 2026.
